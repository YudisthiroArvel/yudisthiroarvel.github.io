## The project

A five-page storefront for a premium audio brand, covering products, deals, an about page and a membership sign-up flow. It was designed in Figma first and only then implemented in HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

## My impact

I did the whole thing alone, from the Figma prototype to the shipped markup. The membership form is where the HCI work actually shows: every field validates on the client with its own inline error message, including a date-of-birth check that computes real age, so a user is corrected where they made the mistake rather than after submitting.

## What I learned

HCI gave me vocabulary for instincts I already had. I learned to defend a layout decision against usability principles instead of saying it looks better, and that an error message placed next to its field is worth more than a perfectly styled page.
