## The research

An IEEE-format research paper proposing a task reminder application that prioritises academic work on its own, instead of leaving students to rank their own deadlines. Existing reminder apps notify reliably, but none of them decide what should be done first.

## My impact

As first author I framed the research problem and ran the literature review across nine prior reminder and productivity systems. Every one of them stopped at recording tasks and firing notifications, and not one applied an algorithm to prioritise by deadline proximity and urgency. That gap became the paper's contribution, and I designed the reminder algorithm and the development stages around closing it.

## What I learned

Writing to IEEE format forced a discipline I had not practised: every claim needs a citation, and a contribution only counts if you can show precisely what nobody else has done. I learned to read a paper for its limitations, not just its results.
