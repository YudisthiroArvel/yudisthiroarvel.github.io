## The contest

The Indonesia National Contest is the qualifying stage of the ICPC Asia Jakarta Regional, held 25 to 26 October 2025. Three people share a single computer for five hours against a set of algorithmic problems. Our team placed in the top 40 and earned an Honorable Mention, qualifying to represent Indonesia under Bina Nusantara University at the regional level.

## My impact

I competed as part of the three-person team, splitting problems by who could see a solution fastest and handing the keyboard over rather than defending it. Placing in the top 40 came down to reading the problem set early and choosing which problems to leave alone.

## What I learned

Contest programming punishes exactly the habits coursework tolerates. With one keyboard between three people, deciding who types next matters as much as knowing the algorithm, and a solution that is correct but too slow is simply wrong.
