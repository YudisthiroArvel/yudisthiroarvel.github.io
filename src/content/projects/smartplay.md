## The concept

A product concept for an interactive educational video app for early-childhood learners. Unlike YouTube Kids or Cocomelon, a Smartplay video pauses to ask the child a question, and parents get a dashboard for screen time, content filters and activity reports, in Indonesian and built around the local PAUD curriculum.

## My impact

I produced the information infographic that carried our concept, condensing the problem, the solution and the user persona into something a reader takes in at a glance. Working from Rina, our persona, kept the team honest: a mother who wanted local-language content she could actually supervise, not another app to police.

## What I learned

This project was less about code and more about whether an idea holds up. Empathy maps, a perceptual map against the apps parents already use, and a revenue and cost structure taught me that a feature is only worth building if someone can explain who pays for it and why.
